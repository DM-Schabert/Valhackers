Rolling Backtest 2016

Ten test periods of three months each, shifted by one month: Jan–Mar to Oct–Dec 2016. Each time, the model trains only on bookings that arrived before the period, and the preprocessing values are learned only from that past as well. We measure the net profit under the game rules, with the same accept rule as in solution.py.

Result across all 10 periods

Gain = net profit minus “reject all” (only the bookings that were already confirmed). Possible gain = “perfect knowledge” minus “reject all”, where perfect knowledge accepts only guests who really show up and still have a free room.

What the numbers say

  • prep_v2 + logreg leads in 9 of 10 periods and is never worse than rejecting everything. Across all periods combined it earns about €227,000 more than full prep + logreg.
  • It is also the most stable: even in its weakest period (Aug–Oct) it captures 54% of the possible gain. Full prep + logreg drops to 33% there, minimal + logreg to 20%, full prep + LightGBM to 6%.
  • The reason is calibration. Every model predicts too few cancellations, but prep_v2 + logreg is on average only 3.5 percentage points below the real rate. Full prep + logreg is 6.8 points below, full prep + LightGBM 12.4. For all of them the gap grows towards the end of the year, because cancellation behaviour shifts over time.
  • Only in May–Jul does another approach lead (minimal + logreg with 67% against 59%). Only €58,000 can be gained in that period, so the differences are small.
  • Our earlier valid and test periods were not lucky picks. In Jul–Sep prep_v2 + logreg captures 81% of the possible gain, in Oct–Dec 74%. That is typical across all periods.
  • Next lever: prep_v2 still underestimates cancellations too, with 32% predicted against 39% actual in Oct–Dec. Better calibration or more weight on recent data should bring further gain.
  • Why 3-month windows: with single months as the test period, nothing can be gained from July to October, not even with perfect knowledge, because the bookings confirmed beforehand fill the hotels completely. The real evaluation probably covers eight months.
